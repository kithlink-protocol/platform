What's inside

Open source, AGPLv3 core · MIT themes · Self-host free forever

🔒

Encrypted by default

Documents sealed with AES-256-GCM. Only shelters reviewing your application can see them — revoke instantly.

🌐

One-click websites

Every shelter gets a free public site on a subdomain or custom domain. Zero code required.

📋

Smart applications

Status pipeline, staff notes, objective checklists. See the whole story before you decide.

📊

Reports that matter

Outcomes, length-of-stay, post-adoption check-ins — exported as CSV for grant writing.

🔄

Syndication built-in

Petfinder sync adapter + RSS feed. Your listings reach more adopters without extra work.

💚

Settling In journeys

Automated day-2 / day-14 / day-30 check-ins after adoption. Gentle, game-like, skippable. Reduces returns.
